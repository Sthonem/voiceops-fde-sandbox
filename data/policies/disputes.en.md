# Transaction Disputes

## Dispute window
You can dispute an unrecognised or incorrect card transaction within 120 days of the transaction date.

## Dispute process
Once a dispute is opened, the review completes within 30 days. The merchant may be asked for documents during this time. You are notified of the outcome by SMS and e-mail.

## Supervisor approval
Disputes for transactions of 10,000 TL or more are approved by a supervisor before the case is opened. Approval is usually given on the same business day.

## Provisional credit
While the review is ongoing, the disputed amount is put on hold on your credit card statement.
