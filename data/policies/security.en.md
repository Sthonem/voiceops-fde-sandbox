# Security and Data Protection

## What the bank never asks for
VoiceOps Demo Bank never asks for your password, card PIN, full card number, CVV or one-time passcode by phone, SMS or e-mail.

## Identity verification
For account-specific information we ask for your registered phone number or customer ID. Sensitive actions such as blocking a card or disputing a transaction also require your date of birth.

## Personal data
Calls are recorded for service quality. Personal data is processed under the Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698). Phone numbers and dates of birth are masked in logs.
