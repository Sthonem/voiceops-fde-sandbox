# Complaints, Callbacks and Service Times

## Complaint response times
Complaints and requests are answered by priority: urgent within 4 hours, high within 24 hours, normal within 48 hours, low within 72 hours.

## Callbacks
Callbacks can be scheduled every day between 09:00 and 18:00, up to 14 days ahead. We call your registered phone number.

## Representatives
Representatives are available every day from 08:00 to 22:00. Lost or stolen card and fraud reports are handled 24/7 with priority.
