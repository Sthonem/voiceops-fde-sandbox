# Money Transfers

## Cut-off times
EFT transfers placed on weekdays between 09:00 and 17:00 are processed the same day. EFT orders placed after 17:00 or at weekends are sent at 09:00 on the next business day. FAST transfers are instant, 24/7. Transfers between VoiceOps Demo Bank accounts always arrive instantly.

## Transfer fees
Internal transfers from the mobile app or internet banking are free. FAST and EFT cost 5 TL in the mobile app and 25 TL at a branch. Premium customers pay no EFT fee.

## Limits
A single FAST transfer can be at most 100,000 TL. The daily mobile transfer limit is 250,000 TL in total. To raise a limit, you must be verified and then speak to a representative.

## Failed transfers
If the recipient IBAN is invalid the transfer is rejected and no money leaves your account. Transfers that fail for insufficient funds are not retried automatically. Transfers under security review usually complete within 2 hours.
