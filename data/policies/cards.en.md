# Cards

## Lost or stolen card
If your card is lost or stolen, block it immediately. After your identity is verified and you confirm, the voice assistant blocks the card permanently and instantly. A blocked card cannot be reactivated.

## Replacement card
A replacement for a blocked card is free and is sent to your registered address within 3-5 business days. In urgent cases you can collect a card from a branch on the same day.

## Annual card fee
Debit cards have no annual fee. The credit card annual membership fee is 750 TL and is waived for premium customers.

## Credit card limit
Limit increase requests are assessed against proof of income and the decision is sent by SMS within 2 business days.
